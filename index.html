🔧 Troubleshooting - Quick Fixes for Common Issues

❌ "The rings don't show my usage"

Check if the app is properly connected. On Windows, you can right-click the tray icon and look for a "Settings" or "Sign in" option. Make sure you're signed into your AI provider's account. If you just installed, give it a minute - it can take a moment for the first sync.

🗔 "I can't find the icon anywhere!"

On Windows, sometimes the system tray is "collapsed" by default. Look for a small "up arrow" icon near the system clock. Click it, and you should see the aiusagebar icon. You can drag it to the visible area if you'd like.

🔄 "The numbers seem old"

Try clicking the icon once to trigger a manual refresh. If that doesn't help, right-click and choose "Refresh" or "Reload" if available.

💬 "I still have a question"

The best place for help is the official repository. Visit the GitHub issues page and search for similar questions or open a new issue. The community and developers are usually pretty responsive.
